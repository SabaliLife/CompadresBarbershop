Good to Know

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through Booksy — pick your barber, pick your time, done in under a minute. Questions first? Call us at (480) 839-4741.

Do you take walk-ins?

We're appointment only — it's how every compa gets a chair on time and unrushed attention in it. Booking on Booksy takes less than a minute, and same-day slots are often available.

How much is a haircut?

A men's haircut is $30, and a haircut with full beard work is $40. Beard shaping and line-ups run $15 on their own. See the full price menu — straightforward pricing, no surprises.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Absolutely — kids are compas too. Cuts for ages 12 and under are $25, and ages 13 and up are $30. Plenty of families make it a group visit.

Where is the shop located?

9206 S. Avenida Del Yaqui, Suite 3, in Guadalupe, AZ — right off the I-10, minutes from Tempe and Arizona Mills. Get directions.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday 9:00a–6:00p, Saturday 8:30a–3:00p, closed Sunday. Saturdays book up quickest — grab your slot early.

Do people really drive in from other cities?

All the time. Compas come from Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, and across the Phoenix Valley — when you find your barber, the drive is part of the ritual. Read the reviews and you'll see why.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash and card both work at the shop. Prices are straightforward with no surprises — and you can meet Martin and Kenny before you ever sit in the chair.
